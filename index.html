Hermes

Personal automation client — single user, non-commercial.

Hermes is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own server. It works with the owner's Google account on the owner's explicit request: reading and organising their own email, calendar, cloud files, spreadsheets and documents.

What it does

  • Reads and searches the owner's Gmail messages and sends messages the owner asks for
  • Reads, creates and removes events in the owner's Google Calendar
  • Lists, downloads and uploads files in the owner's Google Drive
  • Reads and edits the owner's Google Sheets and Google Docs
  • Reads the owner's Google Contacts

Who uses it

One person — the account owner. The application is not offered to the public, has no sign-up process, and is not distributed. There is no server-side service that other people can access.

Data handling

Google user data is accessed only to carry out the owner's own requests. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.
